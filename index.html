FAQ

Good to know before we arrive.

Do you really come to my house?+

Yes. Wash Bros is mobile. We service customers across Durham Region and bring the detailing equipment to you.

What do you need from me?+

For most jobs we need safe access to the vehicle, water, and when required a standard outdoor power outlet.

Why are prices listed as “starting at”?+

A lightly used sedan and a heavily soiled three-row SUV can take very different amounts of time. We quote fairly based on size and condition.

Do you do both interior and exterior?+

Yes. You can book exterior only, interior only, or a full inside-and-out detail.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve Durham Region, including Whitby, Oshawa, Ajax, Pickering, Brooklin and nearby communities.
