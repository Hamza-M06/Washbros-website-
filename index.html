FAQ

BEFORE WE ARRIVE.

What do you need from me?

Safe access to the vehicle, access to water and—depending on the service—a standard outdoor power outlet.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Durham Region, including Whitby, Oshawa, Ajax, Pickering, Brooklin and surrounding communities.

Why are prices “starting at”?

A lightly used sedan and a heavily soiled SUV can require very different amounts of time. We quote based on the actual job.

Do you do interior and exterior?

Yes. Choose exterior only, interior only or a full inside-and-out detail.
